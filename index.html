WHY THE CROSS?

If God is love, why the blood, the sacrifice, and the cross?

Why death? Why blood? Why sacrifice? Those questions can sound troubling until we go back to the beginning. God created life. Sin brought death. The cross has to be understood inside that larger story.

A LIVING TEMPLE

Built on Christ. Made of Living Stones.

Peter calls Jesus the cornerstone and His followers “living stones.” A cornerstone is not decoration. It is laid first, and the rest of the structure is built from it. Peter gives us the same picture of the Church. Christ is the cornerstone, and His people are living stones being fitted together into a spiritual house where God dwells by His Spirit.

BEHOLDING AND BECOMING

What Happens When We Draw Near to God?

Moses came away from the presence of God changed. Paul takes that picture and applies it to us: as we behold the Lord, the Spirit changes us into His image. That makes prayer, abiding, and drawing near far more than religious duties.

THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE

And the Greatest of These Is Love.

We can know the Bible deeply, argue our position well, and still miss the heart of Christ. Paul would not let us forget it: without love, all that knowledge amounts to very little.

Hold tightly to what Scripture makes clear. Be humble about the places faithful Christians have understood differently. And whatever we are discussing, never use truth as an excuse to stop loving the person in front of us.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Let's Return to Jesus Together.

The Way of Christ began with a simple concern: have we made Christianity more complicated in some places and easier in others than Jesus did? This site is an attempt to go back to Jesus and His apostles, listen carefully, and ask what it really means to follow Him.

Some things are more important to us than others. We have been divided by sincere Christians for centuries. We will try to name the differences. We will respect plainly where Scripture is plain, relying on the difference. We will speak plainly where Scripture is plain, but we will never use truth as an excuse to stop loving the very person we may understand or disagree with.
